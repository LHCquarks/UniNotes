In this half of the course we will make a few assumptions to make our problems tractable and learn techniques to solve lots of the left over problems. In general these assumptions are:
- There is no free charges $\rho = 0$
- There is no current $J = 0$
- The Vector nature of light is unimportant
A lot of the time we will also make assumptions about the scale of things ie the screen is really far away.
