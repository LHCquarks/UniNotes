Similar to the electric field we have a "Coulomb" type law with the magnetic field. This law is called the Biot-Savart law and can be expressed in the following ways:
$$
\begin{align}
d\vec B &= \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}I\frac{d\vec s \times\hat {\mathscr r}}{\mathscr r^2} \\
\vec B(\vec r) &= \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\int\frac{\vec I \times\hat {\mathscr r}}{\mathscr r^2}dl' \\
\vec B(\vec r) &= \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\int\frac{\vec K \times\hat {\mathscr r}}{\mathscr r^2}dA' \\
\vec B(\vec r) &= \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\int\frac{\vec J \times\hat {\mathscr r}}{\mathscr r^2}d\tau' \\
\end{align}
$$
