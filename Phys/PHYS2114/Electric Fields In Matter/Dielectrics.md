Dielectrics are **insulators**. Electric charges are bound to their atoms meaning that unlike a conductor the charges are only able to move a little bit.

## The setup
Imagine we have a charged plates setup so that there is an electric field between the two plates. Now lets fill the region in between the two plates with a substance. 
Consider the case of the molecules being:
- **Polar**
- **Non-polar**
![[Pasted image 20260616091756.png]]
### Polar molecules
In the case of polar molecules we get that the negative end of the molecule will be attracted to the positive plate and similar with the positive part of the molecule. This means that the dipoles of the molecules will line up and sum together.
### Non-polar molecules
In the case of non-polar molecules the electron cloud instead will be tugged and thus we get a similar effect as the **polar** molecules
