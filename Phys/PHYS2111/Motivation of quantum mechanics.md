Whist classical physics could explain almost all of the worlds phenomena there were a couple of experimental results that would not go away. These included:
- The specific heat of solids changing at low temperature
- The intensity of light emitted by a black body (**The UV catastrophe**)
- Low frequency light can not eject light from a photo-cathode regardless of the light's intensity (**The photo-electric effect**)
- The scattering of light off electrons in atoms having different a different frequency implying that they have momentum (**The Compton effect**)
- The classical model of an atom predicts that the electrons will fall into the nucleus 
- The **Stern-Gerlach** experiment showed a kind of all or nothing property with the magnetic moment of a particle

These issues lead to the development of the semi-classical model of quantum mechanics.
In this theory classical mechanics still applied however we appended the following equations in a patchwork style:
- $\lambda = \frac{h}{p}$ (**The de Broglie wavelength**)
- $E = hf$ (**The Plank-Einstein relation**)

This however was still not enough and another more powerful theory had to be developed. Enter **Quantum mechanics**
