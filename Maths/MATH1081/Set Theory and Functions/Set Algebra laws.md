Given sets $A, B, C$, the universal set $\mathcal U$ and the empty set $\varnothing$ we have the following identities:
## Comutativity
$$
\begin{align}
A \cup B &= B \cup A \\
A \cap B &= B \cap A
\end{align}
$$
## Associativity
$$
\begin{align}
A \cup (B \cup C) &= (A\cup B) \cup C \\
A \cap (B\cap C) &= (A\cap B) \cap C
\end{align}
$$
## Distributivity
$$
\begin{align}
A \cup (B\cap C) &= (A \cup B) \cap (A\cup C) \\
A \cap (B\cup C) &= (A \cap B) \cup (A\cap C) \\
\end{align}
$$
## Absorption
$$
\begin{align}
A \cup (A\cap B) &= A \\
A \cap (A\cup B) &= A \\
\end{align}
$$
## Idempotence
$$
\begin{align}
A \cup A &= A \\
A \cap A &= A
\end{align}
$$
## Identity
$$
\begin{align}
A \cup \varnothing &= A \\
A \cap \mathcal U &=  A
\end{align}
$$
## Domination
$$
\begin{align}
A \cup \mathcal U &= \mathcal U \\
A \cap \varnothing &= \varnothing
\end{align}
$$
## Complement laws
$$
\begin{align}
A \cup \bar A &= \mathcal U \\
A \cap \bar A &= \varnothing \\
(A^c)^c &= A
\end{align}
$$
## De Morgan's law
$$
\begin{align}
(A \cup B)^c &= A^c \cap B^n \\
(A \cap B)^c &= A^c \cup B^n \\
\end{align}
$$
## Difference laws
$$
\begin{align}
A \backslash B &= A\cap B^c \\
A \ominus B &= (A\cup B) \backslash(A\cap B)
\end{align}
$$
## Inclusion exclusion
$$
\begin{align}
|A \cup B| &= |A| + |B| - |A \cap B|
\end{align}
$$
