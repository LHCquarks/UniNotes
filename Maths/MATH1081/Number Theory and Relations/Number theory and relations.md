## What is Number theory
There is a large amount of number systems, $\mathbb Z, \mathbb Q, \mathbb R, \mathbb C$ to list a few and they all have there own interesting properties. Number theory is the study of $\mathbb Z$ in particular.  
## Why $\mathbb Z$?
We find this set particularly interesting because all but one of our normal operations $+, - , \times, \div$ behave nicely that one being $\div$. In particular all of $+, -$ and $\times$ are **closed** under $\mathbb Z$ which means that two integers remain in the integers under these operations.

Number theory investigates when $\div$ breaks down on $\mathbb Z$ and why.
