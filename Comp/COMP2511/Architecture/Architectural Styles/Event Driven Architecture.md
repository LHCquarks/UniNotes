**Strengths**
- Maintainability
- Performance
- Scalability
- Evolvability
- Fault Tolerance
**Weaknesses**
- Highly complex due to use of asynchronous messaging and parallel event processing
- Testability: Complex event debugging
- Does not work with workflows requiring synchronous, dependent components



Microservices and Event-Drive can and often do appear together, but are conceptually different.
